Questions you may have

Not here? Ask us on the demo call.

Does it work with my website?

Yes, if your website emails you its order notifications. That includes WordPress, Wix and most other platforms. AutoOrders Hub works from the emails you already receive.

Will my customers notice any change?

No. They order exactly as they do today. AutoOrders Hub only improves what happens after the notification reaches your inbox.

Can I add more reps later?

Yes. Add reps as your team grows. Each rep gets a workspace with only the orders assigned to them, while you see everything.

Will I lose my existing order records?

No. During setup we work out with you the best way to handle your existing data.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the size of your business and your workflow. On the demo call we explain the process and give you a realistic timeline.

Is my data secure?

Your operational data stays inside your Google Workspace and the services you already use. You decide what each team member can access.

Do I pay monthly?

No. You pay once. After that, it is yours going forward.
